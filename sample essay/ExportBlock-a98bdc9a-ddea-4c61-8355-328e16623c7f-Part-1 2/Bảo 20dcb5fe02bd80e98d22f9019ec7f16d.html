Topic: Mental health concerns are a growing issue for many people around the world.  What are some reasons for poor mental health, and what can governments do to support citizens’ mental health?

By taking these steps, governments can create a more balanced environment that promotes both productivity and psychological resilience across all age groups.

In conclusion, there are different determinants that lead to the increasing mental health issue worldwide and my belief is that government should implement several practical solutions to alleviate the severe consequences associated with this problem.
